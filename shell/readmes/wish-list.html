Wish List

One wish list for gift giving, not tied to any store. List anything, from any shop or no shop at all, and share it with friends. They reserve what they're getting you so nobody doubles up, and you never find out what's been reserved, so it stays a surprise.

This is a working demo: everything is kept in your browser, there are no passwords, and the demo bar at the top lets you switch between people and move the clock forward to see how things play out.

Trying it out

Sign in as Alice. Her list is already filled in. Bob and Carla are her friends and have reserved a few things; Dan has asked to be her friend. Use Be: in the demo bar to switch between them.

  • As Alice, add an item, then switch to Bob and reserve it. Switch back: Alice's list looks exactly the same.
  • As Carla, open Alice's list: Bob's reservations show as "Reserved by someone", never by whom.
  • As Alice, open Friends and Review Dan's request to see the confirmation step.
  • Mark something No longer want, then use +2 weeks and +6 months to see who still sees it, and for how long.

Your list

Each item has:

  • Item: what it is.
  • Specifics (optional): what a buyer needs to know to get the right one, like a shirt's size and color, or which edition of a book.
  • Est. cost (optional).
  • Link to item (optional): any page that shows the right one, from any store or none. It doesn't have to be where it's bought.
  • Theme (optional): groups items, like Board games, Clothing or BBQ grilling. Friends can filter by theme, and seeing lots of one theme might help someone decide to make a holiday all about it (or do something different).
  • Needs (optional): items this one only makes sense with, like the base game for an expansion. Friends see whether those are reserved, so they know to get this only if the others are being given too. Reserving an item whose needs nobody has reserved asks whether to reserve them all.
  • Add-ons (optional): the other way round, items that go with this one. Pick links from either side: picking B as an add-on of A is the same as picking A as one of B's needs.
  • Who can see: all friends (including friends you add later), all except some, or only the friends you pick, for private or sensitive items.

When you get something, press Received it and it leaves your list.

You can't delete items. Instead, mark one No longer want:

  • It stays on your list for 6 months, marked, in case you change your mind.
  • Whoever reserved it (if anyone) sees a warning for those 6 months: don't buy it, return it, or talk to you. You aren't told whether anyone had.
  • Everyone else sees it marked "No longer wanted" for 2 weeks, then it's gone.

Received items show as received to friends for 2 weeks.

Friends' lists

On a friend's list, search by name or specifics, filter by Theme, Price (under $25 up to $100 and over) and Show (not reserved yet, reserved by you, reserved by anyone, or no longer wanted), and Sort by list order, price either way, name, newest or theme. Items no longer wanted always come last. The filters stay put while you reserve things.

Friends see each other's lists and can reserve items. Reserving means you've bought it or will buy it for the next occasion. The list's owner can't see reservations; other friends see that an item is reserved, but not by whom. Reserved lists everything you've reserved for everyone, with a Bought box only you see, and flags anything no longer wanted.

To add a friend, type their name under Friends. Before the request is sent (and before you accept one), you see every item of yours they'd be able to see, and can untick any you'd rather they didn't. Items you've shared only with certain friends stay hidden from new friends.

The action log

The bar at the bottom lists what a real server would do that the demo can't, like emailing a friend about a request.
