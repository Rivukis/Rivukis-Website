Bus Traffic

A parking-jam puzzle, a take on the mobile game Bus Traffic Fever!: tap buses out of a jammed lot and into the bus stops, so every passenger in the line gets on a bus of their color.

Live at https://rivukis.github.io/Rivukis-Website/bus-traffic/.

How to play

  • The lot is up to 22 squares wide and 26 tall (smaller on easier levels), full of vehicles in up to 10 colors, each with an arrow on its roof. They come in three sizes, all 2 squares wide: small (3 by 2) seats 4, medium (4 by 2) seats 6, and large (5 by 2) seats 10.
  • Tap a vehicle and it drives the way its arrow points. If both lanes in front of it are clear all the way to the edge, it drives out of the lot to a bus stop. If anything is in either lane, it drives up to it, bumps it, honks and backs up.
  • There are 4 stops, filled from left to right: a bus takes the leftmost free stop.
  • The loop runs round the stops, 20 to 30 passengers deep depending on the level, and moves like a conveyor. Anyone in the loop can get on a waiting bus of their color, not just whoever's at the front. They get on the leftmost bus they can first, one at a time: they step off the loop onto the walkway round the stops, walk along it to their bus's door and get in, and a seat on its roof fills. Everyone behind them in the loop steps forward a place.
  • The line waits above the loop, coming out of a tunnel on the right. Each time someone leaves the loop, the front of the line steps down into the loop's entry at the top left, the line shuffles forward, and the next person walks out of the tunnel.
  • A full vehicle leaves, freeing its stop.
  • If every stop holds a bus no one in the loop can take, you're stuck: the level is lost. So the order you tap the buses in matters: a bus whose passengers are still back in the line takes up a stop until they reach the loop.
  • Clear the level by getting everyone on a bus.
  • Every level has been checked by a solver: it can always be won.

Boosters

You start with one of each, and can buy more with coins from the home page.

  • 🚁 Lift: tap it, then tap any bus: it's lifted straight to a stop, whatever's in its way.
  • ➕ Extra stop: one more bus stop for this level.

When you're stuck, you can also watch an ad for one more stop. (Ads and buying coins aren't real yet: they're shown in the action log at the bottom of the page, and nothing is charged.)

You earn 20 coins for clearing a level the first time, 5 for clearing it again.

Packs and levels

There are five Classic packs of 10 levels, one for each difficulty, laid out like the level packs in Flow Free:

Pack Lot Colors
Beginner 14 by 16 6
Easy 18 by 20 8
Medium 22 by 26 10
Hard 22 by 26 10
Very hard 22 by 26 10

The harder the pack, the deeper the chains of cars blocking cars, the fewer cars that can leave at the start, and the more wrong taps there are to avoid. Within a pack, levels get a little harder as you go.

How hard a level is is measured by how hard the right taps are to find: at each step, how many buses could drive out and how many of those would still let you win.

Pick a pack, and its levels are shown in pages of 25. A solved level shows a star; a locked one a padlock. Three levels you haven't solved are always open in each pack, so you can skip a hard one and come back to it. Your progress is saved in your browser.

Testing mode. While the game is being tested, the button at the top switches between testing mode and normal mode, and remembers your choice. In testing mode every level is open, each level shows its numbers (size, buses, colors, how deep the loop is, choices: how hard the right taps are to find, traps: taps that would lose, effort: how many positions the solver looked at, free: cars that can leave at the start, depth: how deep the chains go, keystone: the most cars one car holds up, and fan-in: the most cars in one car's way), a 💡 Solve button plays the solution from wherever you are (tap it again to take over), and after clearing a level you can rate how hard it was. The ratings are listed on the home page to copy and send back, to tune the levels. A Testing panel at the top of every screen has settings to try: turning the lot's grid lines off and on, and a slider for how fast tapped vehicles drive (remembered in your browser, with a button back to the default).

The speaker button at the top turns sound off and on.
