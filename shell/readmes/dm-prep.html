DM Prep

A prep and play companion for Dungeon Masters. Write up your adventures (room by room), NPCs, monsters, quests, buildings, towns, factions, items, encounters and the players' characters; star what you need for the next session; run the session from one page; and print any of it as cut-out cards.

The tabs at the top are Session (home), one per kind of card, Cheatsheet, and Your data (export and import). The search box searches everything (press Ctrl+K or / to jump to it).

Sessions

The Session tab is for the game in front of you.

  • Starred cards. Every card has two stars: Every session (always on hand) and Session N (just this one). Starred cards are listed at the top of the Session tab, and Print starred cards prints them all.
  • Adventures, room by room. A starred adventure gets its own tab next to ★ Starred. Pick the room the party is in, and you see just that room's enemies (with their stats and pictures), NPCs, items and notes. Pick more rooms when the neighbors hear the fight and come running.
  • Encounters on the fly. The ⚔ Encounters tab, next to the others, lists every encounter: backups, random encounters on the road, anything not tied to a room. Filter by name, place or tag (say "road"), show only the ones not in an adventure yet, pick one or more to see their enemies' stats, tactics and treasure, or press 🎲 Random encounter to roll one from the list.
  • Info and quests. Every NPC's info (rumors, things they know) and quests are listed here too, starred NPCs first. Roll a rumor picks one the players haven't heard yet.
  • What the players have been told. Each info and quest item has a box: empty (not told), a green ✓ (told), or ◐ (partly told, with a note on what was said). Tap it to change it. A small number on the box says how many sessions ago it happened; no number means this session.
  • Start session when you sit down to play, and jot notes in the Session log as you go (names in notes link to their cards).
  • End session asks which of this session's starred cards carry over to the next one, and for your notes on the session. Past sessions keep their notes and log, at the bottom of the tab.

Cards

Every card has its own page to fill in, with a live preview of the printed card, and a View page made for the table: big text, links, and the told boxes.

Links. Wherever a card's name appears in another card (an NPC's Location, a building's Workers, a rumor), it links to that card, and each card lists where it's Mentioned. Fields that name other cards suggest names as you type; a name that isn't a card yet gets a + button that makes it one, already linked back.

Dice. 🎲 buttons roll a name (by species and gender), species, quirk, motivation and pitfall, a voice, or a building or town name. Quick NPC rolls a half-made NPC (pick an archetype like Guard or Innkeeper for their job and stat block too) for when the players talk to someone you didn't plan for.

Tags and filtering. Give cards tags (comma-separated) and filter a list by name, text or tag, or show just the starred ones.

Pictures. Monsters and player characters can have a picture; it shows on their pages and on the Session tab (not on the printout).

NPC cards

  • Name, Species, Age, Gender, Size, then Profession, Quirk, Stat block, then Goal, Motivation, Pitfall, then Location, Faction.
  • Motivation and Pitfall are picked from twelve traits, with help on how each wins the NPC over or insults them.
  • Info and Quests: lists, with a told box on each item (the printed card has a tick box per item). Each quest line links to a quest card, with an optional line of your own.
  • Background and Notes.
  • Voice: nine boxes (Spatial, Timing, Weight, Tempo, Volume, Tone, Location, Breath, # Words) and a wide Defect box. Spatial, Timing and Weight name a Laban voice style, with characters who sound like it. A box with nothing picked prints blank, to fill in by hand.

Adventures

An adventure has a name, where it is, a summary, notes, and rooms. Each room has a name, its enemies, NPCs, items, encounters and notes. Each enemy has its own boxes for how many, which monster (or NPC) and an optional note ("Ripper", "leader"). Put a prepped encounter in a room and its enemies join the room's. Names are suggested as you type and link to their cards.

Quests

Quests are cards of their own: name, who gives it, status, where, reward, description and notes. On an NPC, each line of Quests names a quest (linked, suggested as you type, or made on the spot) and, if you like, a line in your own words for how this NPC puts it. The told box is on each NPC's line, so you know who has passed it on.

Monsters

Name, type, CR, AC, HP, speed, stat block, attacks and abilities, tactics and notes, and a picture.

Other cards

  • Buildings: name, town, description, workers, backstory, notes.
  • Towns: name, region, size, leader, description, notes.
  • Factions: name, leader, goal, members, allies, enemies, description, notes.
  • Items: name, type, rarity, value, who has it, description, notes.
  • Encounters: name, location, difficulty, enemies (how many, which monster, a note), tactics, treasure, notes. Use tags like "road" or "travel" to group them for the Session tab.
  • Players: character, player, species, class and level, hooks, notes, and a picture.

Printing

Tick the cards you want in a list and press Download PDF; every download shows its pages first, where you pick the paper (Letter or A4) and cards per page. As many as fit (the default) makes each card as tall as what's on it; or pick 3 or 4 to a page. Download a blank page gives empty cards to fill in by hand. Print at 100% and cut along the dashed lines.

The Cheatsheet tab has the motivations and pitfalls, the Laban voice styles and every voice option, with a button to download it as a PDF.

Keeping your cards

Everything is saved in this browser only. There's no account or server yet, so clearing your browser's data, or using another browser or device, starts from scratch. After your first visit, the site works offline too.

On the Your data tab, Export all downloads one file with every card and session. Keep it somewhere safe. Import file… reads an export back in: cards from the file are added, and any card that's already here is replaced by the file's copy. Nothing is deleted by an import.

On a first visit, a few sample cards from Phandalin are there to show how things work. Delete them whenever you like.
